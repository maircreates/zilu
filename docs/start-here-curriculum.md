# ZiLu Start Here Curriculum

## Purpose

Start Here is the bridge for learners with zero knowledge of Chinese. It prepares them to enter the Volume 2 pathway without assuming they completed Volume 1. Learners meet sound and meaning before being asked to remember written forms.

All learner-facing Chinese uses Traditional Chinese. Every lesson uses original ZiLu explanations and activities rather than reproducing reference-book text.

## Learning sequence

### 1. Your first greeting

- Phrase: `你好`
- Learn that Chinese characters carry meaning and pinyin guides pronunciation.
- Hear and recognize `nǐ hǎo`.
- Notice the natural spoken change to `ní hǎo` without requiring terminology.
- Complete a meaning-recognition and recall check.

### 2. Your name and identity

- Core language: `我`, `你`, `是`, `不是`, `我叫…`
- Say “I am…,” “I am not…,” and “My name is…”.
- Learn the basic subject–verb order through examples rather than grammar labels.

### 3. The sound map

- Understand initials, finals, and syllables.
- Distinguish a small, high-value set of contrasting sounds.
- Learn how pinyin spelling differs from English spelling.
- Use listen-and-choose activities before speaking tasks.

### 4. Tones without fear

- Meet the four tones and neutral tone through voice movement and listening.
- Identify tone differences in familiar syllables.
- Learn that tone changes can occur naturally in connected speech.
- Avoid isolated tone-drilling without meaning.

### 5. Numbers that unlock daily life

- Core language: `一` through `十`, plus zero and common combinations.
- Recognize and say phone numbers, prices, ages, dates, and times.
- Introduce number gestures and culturally useful conventions where relevant.

### 6. People and simple questions

- Core language: `他`, `她`, `誰`, `嗎`, `呢`.
- Ask and answer simple identity questions.
- Understand that spoken `他` and `她` sound the same.
- Build confidence with short two-person exchanges.

### 7. Wants, likes, and needs

- Core language: `要`, `想`, `喜歡`, `不`, `很`.
- Express a want, preference, and simple negative.
- Learn that Chinese does not conjugate verbs like English.
- Assemble short original sentences from known pieces.

### 8. Time and routines

- Core language for today, tomorrow, days, clock time, and common activities.
- Read simple time expressions in the correct order.
- Ask when something happens.
- Prepare for scheduling and birthday content later in Volume 2.

### 9. Essential survival interactions

- Core phrases: `謝謝`, `不客氣`, `對不起`, `沒關係`, `請`, `再見`.
- Choose phrases by situation rather than translate word by word.
- Practice polite repair when communication breaks down.

### 10. Ready for the ZiLu pathway

- Combine greetings, identity, questions, wants, numbers, and time.
- Complete a short guided conversation.
- Review weak items using recognition and recall.
- Unlock the Volume 2 pathway when the learner can understand the core exchange with support available.

## Lesson rhythm

Every Start Here lesson follows the same six-stage rhythm:

1. **Meet** — encounter one useful piece of language in context.
2. **Listen** — connect the language to sound before analysis.
3. **Notice** — see only the character, pronunciation, or pattern details needed now.
4. **Practice** — make a supported choice with immediate, explanatory feedback.
5. **Recall** — retrieve meaning or language with less support.
6. **Review** — see what was learned and what comes next.

## Completion standard

A lesson is complete when the learner has visited all six stages, attempted its supported practice, and answered its recall check correctly. Completion measures readiness to continue, not permanent mastery. ZiLu will revisit material later through spaced review.

## Entry into Volume 2

Start Here does not attempt to recreate all of Volume 1. It supplies the minimum language, pronunciation habits, interface familiarity, and learner confidence needed before Volume 2 Lesson 11. Any missing prerequisite discovered during Volume 2 development should become a short bridge lesson or just-in-time explanation.
